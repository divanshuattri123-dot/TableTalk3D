# Store listing draft — review before publication

App name: **Table Talk: City Game**

Short description:

Trade, build and play with friends. Share a room code on Android and PC.

Full description:

Bring your people to the table. Table Talk is a property-trading board game for
two to six friends, with a colourful 3D city and an easy room-code invitation.

Create a room, share its four-letter code and play together from separate
devices. Android and PC players use the same table. Internet is required for
multiplayer.

Choose Indian city boards or other city editions. Buy deeds, collect rent, build
houses and hotels, enter auctions and negotiate trades with counter-offers.
Choose the 40-space Classic rules option or an expanding board for a larger group.

The mobile game is designed for landscape play, with touch controls and game
preferences in Menu. Confirmed moves are kept in the room so a returning player
can reconnect to their saved seat on the same installation.

Core multiplayer is free. Cosmetic options do not change dice results, rent or
starting cash. The test version contains explicitly labelled free preview unlocks;
real purchases and live ads are not connected in that version.

Use a nickname, not personal contact details. Other people with the room code can
see player nicknames and game actions. Children should play with people they know.
