## Release path

The current APK is a debug-signed family preview. It is suitable for direct test
installation, not a production Play submission. The store currently has free test
unlocks; live billing, cloud ownership and ad serving are disconnected.

1. Complete phone QA and resolve release-blocking defects.
2. Establish commercial rights to supplied APK-derived models/audio or replace
   them. New generated roadside leaf art is recorded separately. Do not describe
   Table Talk as Hasbro's official app or use unlicensed branding in the listing.
   [Google Play intellectual-property policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9888072).
3. The owner creates/verifies the Play developer account and accepts its terms.
   Google currently lists a US$25 one-time registration fee, separate from the
   ₹5,000 ad budget. The owner handles identity and payment details.
   [Play Console setup](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6112435).
4. Prepare the production upload key, signed Android App Bundle, final privacy
   policy/support contact, Data Safety answers, content rating and listing assets.
   Do not publish placeholder identity or contact information.
5. Start internal testing, then the required closed test for applicable new personal
   accounts: at least 12 opted-in testers continuously for 14 days, followed by an
   application for production access. This is a Google requirement, not an
   instant-publishing option.
   [Current testing requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en).
6. If real ads or purchases are enabled, verify receipt restoration and the mixed
   child/adult audience implementation before submission. Keep guest core play
   available. Do not charge through the local preview wallet.
   [Families policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9893335).
7. Review the final production listing, package and paused campaign together.
   Submit/activate only when account access, rights, QA and the approved budget
   controls are in place. Track Play review status; approval is not guaranteed.

## Regional evidence and recommendation

The public review corpus cannot identify the country where the Android Classic
game is played most. Its country query parameter does not identify reviewer
residence. MONOPOLY GO download/revenue reports describe a different game.

Sensor Tower's public **iOS publisher portfolio** overview lists US, UK and Turkey
among Marmalade's leading regions. This is only a demand proxy: it is neither a
current Android Classic DAU ranking nor a country breakdown for this app.
[Source](https://app.sensortower.com/publisher/ios/988365603/).

Proposed first experiment: **India, US and UK**, separate campaigns and results.
India fits the current Indian boards and the developer's local tester network;
US/UK test English-speaking board-game demand. This is a test recommendation,
not a claim that these are the three most-played Android regions. Revisit it with
current Play Console acquisition/retention results and campaign forecasts once
accounts are available. Do not spend the full amount based on review volume.

## ₹5,000 spend envelope

| Allocation | Maximum planned media cost |
|---|---:|
| India initial test | ₹1,200 |
| US initial test | ₹800 |
| UK initial test | ₹800 |
| Reserve for the best measured region | ₹1,200 |
| Tax/fee/unspent buffer | ₹1,000 |
| Total authorized ceiling | **₹5,000** |

The media subtotal is ₹4,000; the buffer is not permission to add spend. Check the
actual billing-country tax treatment before launch. Reduce media if taxes/fees
would exceed the remaining buffer. Do not treat an average daily budget as a
hard campaign-total ceiling: Google can spend above that average on a given day.
For a small fixed-budget search test, use supported campaign-total budgets and
explicit start/end dates where available. Do not assume App campaigns support
the same total-budget controls. Leave all campaigns paused until reviewed.
[Google budget guidance](https://support.google.com/google-ads/faq/10286469?hl=en).

Use contextual, adult-facing family-game messaging. No personalized targeting of
children, purchased customer lists or remarketing audience built from minors.
[Google's child-ad restrictions](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7341547?hl=en).

## First test design

- Run a seven-day initial pilot after a stable production listing exists.
- Use actual gameplay screenshots/video; show the real Unity version.
- Keep country, creative, dates, billed media and tax totals in one ledger.
- Measure Play installs and retained installers by country; use tester feedback
  for room joining and complete matches. In-game attribution is not implemented
  in the current preview, so do not report invented room-start conversions.
- Review results before using the ₹1,200 reserve. Stop on a crash/reconnect
  regression, misleading creative, billing problem or a risk of exceeding ₹5,000.
- A small pilot is directional evidence; it cannot promise earnings, a reliable
  lifetime-value estimate or the world's most popular region.
